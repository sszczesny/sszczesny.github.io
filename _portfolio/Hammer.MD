I work in the Vanderbilt Machine Shop, and work to assist and teach students how to use the available machines for personal, extracurricular, and academic projects. I TA ME 1151, Introduction to Machining, and lead students in learning metalworking, measuring specifications, machining safety, and general shop knowledge. Their final project is using the mill and lathe to machine a metal hammer.
